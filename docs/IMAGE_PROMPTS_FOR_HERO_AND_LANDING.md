# Image prompts for hero slides & landing page (Google Flow AI / AI image tools)

Use these prompts to generate hero carousel images and supporting visuals for the Studio CAS Settore Legale di Souiai SNC landing page. They are written for **Google Flow AI** or similar text-to-image tools. Generate in **high resolution**; you can crop for mobile later or request variants.

**Brand context:** Studio CAS Settore Legale di Souiai SNC — Centro di Ascolto. Social assistance, information and legal consultation for migrants. Professional, trustworthy, welcoming. Colors: deep teal/blue, warm neutrals (stone, cream). Italian city (Ancona), EU/inclusion theme.

---

## Hero slide images (main carousel)

**Aspect ratio:** Prefer **16:9** or **3:1** for desktop; same image often cropped to **4:5** or **9:16** for mobile (keep important elements in the center third so they survive cropping).

---

### Hero 1 — Welcome / trust

```
Professional hero image for a nonprofit legal and social assistance office in Italy. Warm, calm scene: a modern welcoming office or community space with soft natural light, empty desks or a small meeting table, plants, Italian and EU documents or folders subtly visible. Deep teal and cream color accents. Clean, inclusive, trustworthy mood. No people's faces; focus on space and atmosphere. Photorealistic, high quality, suitable for a website hero banner. Wide aspect ratio 16:9, composition with main interest in the center so it works when cropped for mobile.
```

Photorealistic, cinematic, suitable for website hero. 16:9, key elements in center third for mobile crop.

- Same style and mood as the hero prompts above.
- **Aspect ratio: 9:16** (portrait).
- Main subject or focal point in the **center** of the frame so it fits a tall mobile hero without losing the message.

Example for Hero 1:

```
Same as [Hero 1 prompt above], but portrait orientation 9:16 for mobile hero. All important elements in the vertical center so it works on phone screens.
```

## Quick checklist

- **Hero:** 16:9 or 3:1, center-weighted so cropping to 4:5 or 9:16 on mobile keeps the message.
- **Sections:** 4:3 or 1:1 for cards and grids on mobile and desktop.
- **Style:** Photorealistic, professional, warm. Teal + cream/stone. No identifiable faces; focus on spaces, objects, light.
- **Mood:** Trustworthy, welcoming, inclusive, Italian/EU, nonprofit/social assistance.

After generating, export at **high resolution** (e.g. 1920×1080 for hero, 1200×900 for cards) and compress with TinyPNG or similar for the web.
